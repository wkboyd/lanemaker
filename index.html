Quick start
  • Lane Tool: click to place points, double-click to finish.
  • Click a segment to select it. Drag the blue handles on its sides to add or remove lanes.
  • Hover a segment end: + adds one lane, ➜ continues the whole road.
  • Right-click a lane for markings, paint, features and tram rail.
  • Rail Tool: draws standalone track.
