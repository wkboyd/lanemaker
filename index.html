Quick start

Draw roads lane by lane on a real map, then export a picture.

  • Lane Tool: click points, double-click to finish. Select a segment and drag its blue handles to add lanes.
  • Right-click for markings, arrows, signs and crosswalks. R flips a lane.
  • Intersection Tool joins lane ends; Box / Lasso multi-select.
  • Share exports a PNG; Save keeps the project.
